About Me

Public finance analyst focused on the drivers of state and local government credit quality—liability structures, fiscal capacity, and long-run budget sustainability. I work with government financial data to analyze expenditure trends and unfunded pension and OPEB liabilities. At Fairfax County's Department of Management and Budget, I monitor pension and OPEB trust funds and model the cost of collective bargaining proposals—work at the intersection of budgeting, liability management, and fiscal risk.
