About Me

Independent economist and data builder working at the intersection of applied econometrics and economic data infrastructure. I develop platforms that transform raw government microdata into rigorous, interpretable analysis — and publish research on how policy shapes distributional outcomes across markets and populations. My work spans macroeconomic data engineering, causal inference, and the kind of close reading of economic indicators that aggregate figures tend to obscure.
