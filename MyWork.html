Economics & Market Research

Magnastats — Labor Market Analytics Platform

An end-to-end economic data platform powered by Python pipelines pulling from the BLS Current Population Survey (12.7 million records), JOLTS, and the FRED API across 35 macroeconomic series. Ingests, cleans, and weights raw government microdata using pandas and pyarrow, computes demographic cuts across gender, education, age, and sector, and classifies macroeconomic regimes using a six-state Hidden Markov Model trained on labor and inflation indicators.

Data is refreshed monthly and exported to optimized JSON for a React frontend — no database, no vendor lock-in, just public data made usable.

Market Pulse — Real-Time Macroeconomic Sentiment Aggregator

A real-time market intelligence dashboard tracking over 600 daily headlines across premier financial and policy outlets to quantify overall market momentum. Stories are categorized into dedicated Market, Economy, and Policy verticals, with a custom "Market Mood" index tracked over time — turning qualitative news flow into a measurable signal.

Demonstrates applied fluency in econometrics and market microstructure analysis, interpreting fiscal and monetary signals such as Federal Reserve transitions and geopolitical energy shocks, alongside business intelligence tracking of enterprise M&A and strategic acquisitions.
