Economics & Market Research

Magnastats — Economic Data & Analytics Platform

An end-to-end economic data platform powered by Python pipelines pulling from the BLS Current Population Survey (12.7 million records), JOLTS, and the FRED API across 35 macroeconomic series. Ingests, cleans, and weights raw government microdata using pandas and pyarrow, computes demographic cuts across gender, education, age, and sector, and classifies macroeconomic regimes using a six-state Hidden Markov Model trained on labor and inflation indicators.

Data is refreshed monthly and exported to optimized JSON for a React frontend — no database, no vendor lock-in, just public data made usable.

Econometrics (Academic) Research

The Review of International Organizations  ·  Open Access

The Unintended Consequences of IMF Programs: Women Left Behind in the Labor Market

Published May 2024 Volume 20, pp. 741–767 (2025) 6,604 Accesses 17 Citations

Examines how IMF austerity and liberalization programs affect women's economic empowerment in developing countries. Using a panel of 128 countries from 1992–2018 and an error-correction model with instrumental-variable design, the paper finds that IMF programs unconditionally widen the gender unemployment gap — women are disproportionately displaced regardless of societal norms. Effects on labor force participation are heterogeneous: adverse impacts are amplified in countries where discriminatory gender norms are deeply ingrained.

Working Paper

Pay Transparency and the Gender Mobility Gap: Evidence from Staggered State-Level Adoption

CPS Data 2018–2025 JEL: J16, J31, J63, J78, K31

Studies how pay transparency laws affect job mobility by gender. Using CPS data and staggered state-level adoption of salary-range disclosure mandates, shows that these laws increase job switching substantially more for men than for women — a differential of 3.2 percentage points, representing an 18% increase over baseline male switching rates. Results suggest pay transparency policies may generate indirect distributional effects that run counter to their intended equity objectives.
