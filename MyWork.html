Magnastats — Labor Market Analytics Platform

Built an end-to-end economic data platform powered by Python pipelines pulling from the Bureau of Labor Statistics Current Population Survey (12.7 million records), JOLTS, and the FRED API across 35 macroeconomic series. The backend ingests, cleans, and weights raw government microdata using pandas and pyarrow, computes demographic cuts across gender, education, age, and sector, and classifies macroeconomic regimes using a six-state Hidden Markov Model trained on labor and inflation indicators. All data is refreshed monthly and exported to optimized JSON for a React frontend — no database, no vendor lock-in, just public data made usable.

The analytical layer reflects a deliberate economics perspective: the platform tracks not just headline figures but the distributional story underneath them — who is gaining, who isn't, and why the aggregate obscures it. Indicators like the quits rate, yield curve spread, and SLOOS lending standards sit alongside CPS wage cuts by education and state, enabling a richer read on labor market conditions than any single data source allows.
